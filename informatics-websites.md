# Informatics Websites and Resources

## Where to find the tools, training and help JIC Informatics offers

**Time:** about 15 minutes
**Level:** Beginner — no prior experience needed

By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to:

- Find the Informatics HUB and the tools linked from it
- Check what your HPC jobs cost and how much storage your group uses
- Know where the training material lives
- Find the research databases and tools hosted at JIC
- Find the HPC services run by Research Computing
- Know how to get help from the Informatics team

The main idea to take away:

> **Start at [informatics.jic.ac.uk](https://informatics.jic.ac.uk). Every Informatics tool is linked from there, and if what you need isn't, email informatics@jic.ac.uk or come to a drop-in session.**

---

## 1. The Informatics HUB

[informatics.jic.ac.uk](https://informatics.jic.ac.uk) is the front page for everything the Informatics team runs. The bar at the top switches between the tools, and the page lists what the team offers: consultancy, training, data management and website hosting.

Some tools are marked **Experimental**. They work and you can use them, but they are newer and may still change.

## 2. Your HPC jobs and storage

| Site | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [HPC Job Stats](https://job-stats.informatics.jic.ac.uk) | What your Slurm jobs cost, how much of the CPU and memory you asked for they actually used, and your history by month. It also has a cost and CO2 estimator, and a script builder that writes the `#SBATCH` lines for you. |
| [Great Data Management](https://jic-datamanagement.informatics.jic.ac.uk) | How much storage each group and project uses, how much of it nobody has opened in years, and how it grows over time. |

Both log you in with your normal JIC username and password.

> **Before submitting a big job**, try the script builder in HPC Job Stats. It warns you if the walltime is longer than the partition allows.

## 5. Research databases and tools hosted at JIC

Informatics hosts these for JIC groups and their collaborators. Most are public and cited in papers.

**Gene expression**

| Site | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [wheat-expression.com](https://www.wheat-expression.com) | expVIP for wheat: compare a gene's expression across hundreds of published RNA-seq samples (tissues, development, stress). |
| [rust-expression.com](https://www.rust-expression.com) | expVIP for the wheat rust fungi, across published infection studies. |
| [bachberryexp.com](https://www.bachberryexp.com) | expVIP for blackberry and raspberry fruit ripening. |
| [pea-expression.jic.ac.uk](https://pea-expression.jic.ac.uk) | expVIP for pea (in development). |
| [ORDER](https://order.jic.ac.uk) | Oilseed rape (*Brassica napus*) expression during the floral transition. |
| [Seed ORDER](https://seed-order-brassica.jic.ac.uk) | *Brassica* expression during seed maturation. |
| [Wheat Spatial](https://www.wheat-spatial.com) | Spatial transcriptomics (MERFISH, 200 genes) of the developing wheat inflorescence. |
| [GrainX](https://grainx.jic.ac.uk) | Spatial transcriptomics (Stereo-seq) of wheat grains (cv. Cadenza) at 12 and 18 days after flowering. |

**Sequences, genomes and markers**

| Site | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [blast.jic.ac.uk](https://blast.jic.ac.uk) | BLAST against about 150 JIC databases (wheat, pea, grass pea, brassica, yeast and others). |
| [PolyMarker](https://polymarker.jic.ac.uk) | Designs homoeologue-specific SNP primers for polyploids such as wheat. |
| [Crop Haplotypes](https://crop-haplotypes.com) | Haplotype blocks across wheat pangenome cultivars, chromosome by chromosome. |
| [Brassica rapa TILLING](https://tilling.jic.ac.uk) | Search the *B. rapa* TILLING population for mutations in your gene. |
| [RevGen UK](https://revgenukorders.jic.ac.uk) | Search for and order TILLING mutants from the RevGen UK service. |
| [peapollo](https://peapollo.informatics.jic.ac.uk) | Pea genome browser (JBrowse). |
| [G4Atlas](https://g4atlas.org) | Database of RNA G-quadruplexes across species. |

**Other research tools**

| Site | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [OMERO](https://omero.nbi.ac.uk) | Store, view and share microscopy images (login needed). |
| [Mascot](https://mascot.jic.ac.uk) | Protein identification from mass spectrometry data. |
| [DEDAL](https://dedal.jic.ac.uk) | Drone image processing, reflectance calibration and plot data, for UK academics and farmers (account needed). |
| [Aeciospore Dispersal Model](https://aeciospore-dispersal-model.com) | Predicts how stem rust spores spread from barberry. |
| [Chronotopia](https://chronotopia.informatics.jic.ac.uk) | Interactive exploration of time-series data. |
| [streptomyces.org.uk](https://streptomyces.org.uk) | Streptomyces resources: genome database (StrepDB), the JIC strain collection and links. |
| [ActinoBase](https://actinobase.org) | Community wiki of protocols for actinomycete bacteria. |
| [AntSpec](https://www.antspec.org) | Data on *Antirrhinum* species, hybrid zones and genetics. |
| [Coen Lab software](https://coensoft.jic.ac.uk) | Software and models from the Coen lab (e.g. treeXY). |

> **A link in a paper doesn't work?** Old services from `jicbio.nbi.ac.uk` have been retired or moved. Email informatics@jic.ac.uk and we will try to find the data or restore the service.

## 7. Getting help

- **Email:** [informatics@jic.ac.uk](mailto:informatics@jic.ac.uk)
- **Drop-in sessions:** every Wednesday, 11:00–12:00, at the REC (Bioinformatics, Python Surgery and AI Clinic). For anything that needs digging, email your question beforehand so we can bring a proper answer.
- **In person:** the Informatics team is on the second floor of Bateson.
- **Code:** the team's public projects are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Informatics-John-Innes-Centre).
